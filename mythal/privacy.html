Mythal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2025

Mythal ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

By using the Mythal application, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address — for account creation and authentication
  • Apple ID / Google ID token — for social sign-in (we store only the subject identifier, not your Apple/Google password)
  • Nickname — your display name within the app
  • Language preference — for interface localization

1.2 Usage Data

  • Chat messages — prompts you send and AI-generated responses, stored for conversation history and service improvement
  • Character data — characters you create, including names, descriptions, and AI-generated visuals
  • Interaction logs — session timestamps, features used, and error reports

1.3 Transaction Data

  • Purchase receipts — Apple App Store transaction IDs and receipts for subscription and Gem management
  • Payment records — order IDs, product IDs, and transaction amounts (processed by Apple; we do not store credit card or payment details)

1.4 Technical Data

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • IP address (for security and regional model selection)
  • Country code (from device locale, for content regionalization)
  • Network type (WiFi/cellular)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide the Service — authenticate your identity, process conversations, generate AI content, and manage your account
  • Process transactions — verify purchases, manage subscriptions, and credit Gems
  • Improve the Service — analyze usage patterns, debug issues, and develop new features
  • Ensure safety — detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Communicate — send service-related notifications (e.g., subscription renewals, policy updates)

3. AI Processing & Your Data

When you interact with AI characters, your messages are sent to third-party AI model providers for processing:

  • Text generation — powered by registered LLM providers (e.g., Alibaba Cloud, DeepSeek) using OpenAI-compatible API protocols
  • Image generation — processed by AI image model providers (e.g., FLUX, Wan, xAI Grok)
  • Video generation — processed by AI video model providers
  • Speech synthesis (TTS) — processed by text-to-speech service providers

These providers process your data under their own privacy policies and data handling practices. We select providers that commit to not using API inputs for model training. We do not send personally identifiable information (such as your email or name) to these AI providers — only the content of your prompts and conversation context.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers — with third-party companies that help us operate the Service (AI model providers, cloud hosting, payment processors), under contractual obligations to protect your data
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Safety — to protect the rights, property, or safety of Mythal, our users, or the public
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Specifically:

  • Account data — retained while the account is active
  • Chat history — retained for the duration of each conversation, subject to context window limits
  • Transaction records — retained for at least 7 years as required by financial regulations
  • AI usage records — retained for billing audit and service improvement

When you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext
  • Authentication tokens are securely managed with automatic expiration
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (via the "Delete Account" feature in the app)
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdrawal of consent — you may stop using the Service and delete your account at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@mythal.app.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us at support@mythal.app.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Our servers are located in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

We ensure that international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Apple App Store — for app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Sign in with Apple — for Apple ID authentication
  • Google Sign-In — for Google account authentication
  • AI model providers — for content generation (text, image, video, speech)
  • Cloud hosting — for server infrastructure and data storage

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Cookies & Tracking

The Mythal mobile app does not use browser cookies. We may use device identifiers and analytics tokens for app performance monitoring and crash reporting.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking in the mobile application.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or via email. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates when the latest revision was made.

Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: support@mythal.app

Mythal 隐私政策

生效日期：2025 年 9 月 1 日

Mythal（"我们"）尊重您的隐私，致力于保护您与我们分享的个人信息。本隐私政策说明了我们收集哪些信息、如何使用这些信息，以及您拥有的选择权。

使用 Mythal 应用即表示您同意按照本政策所述方式收集和使用您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 账户信息

  • 邮箱地址 — 用于账户创建和身份验证
  • Apple ID / Google ID token — 用于社交登录（我们仅存储 subject 标识符，不存储您的 Apple/Google 密码）
  • 昵称 — 您在应用内的显示名称
  • 语言偏好 — 用于界面本地化

1.2 使用数据

  • 聊天消息 — 您发送的提示词和 AI 生成的回复，用于对话历史和服务改进
  • 角色数据 — 您创建的角色，包括名称、描述和 AI 生成的视觉内容
  • 交互日志 — 会话时间戳、使用的功能和错误报告

1.3 交易数据

  • 购买收据 — Apple App Store 交易 ID 和收据，用于订阅和宝石管理
  • 支付记录 — 订单 ID、商品 ID 和交易金额（由 Apple 处理；我们不存储信用卡或支付详情）

1.4 技术数据

  • 设备型号、操作系统版本和应用版本
  • IP 地址（用于安全和区域模型选择）
  • 国家/地区代码（来自设备语言设置，用于内容区域化）
  • 网络类型（WiFi/蜂窝网络）

2. 我们如何使用您的信息

我们将收集的信息用于：

  • 提供服务 — 验证您的身份、处理对话、生成 AI 内容和管理您的账户
  • 处理交易 — 验证购买、管理订阅和发放宝石
  • 改进服务 — 分析使用模式、排查问题和开发新功能
  • 保障安全 — 检测和防止欺诈、滥用和安全事件
  • 通信 — 发送服务相关通知（如订阅续订、政策更新）

3. AI 处理与您的数据

当您与 AI 角色互动时，您的消息会被发送到第三方 AI 模型提供商进行处理：

  • 文本生成 — 由已注册的 LLM 提供商（如阿里云百炼、DeepSeek）通过 OpenAI 兼容 API 协议处理
  • 图片生成 — 由 AI 图片模型提供商（如 FLUX、Wan、xAI Grok）处理
  • 视频生成 — 由 AI 视频模型提供商处理
  • 语音合成 (TTS) — 由文字转语音服务提供商处理

这些提供商根据其自身的隐私政策和数据处理实践处理您的数据。我们选择的提供商承诺不将 API 输入用于模型训练。我们不会向这些 AI 提供商发送个人身份信息（如您的邮箱或姓名）——仅发送您的提示词和对话上下文内容。

4. 数据共享

我们不会将您的个人信息出售给第三方。

我们仅在以下情况下可能共享您的信息：

  • 服务提供商 — 与帮助我们运营服务的第三方公司（AI 模型提供商、云托管、支付处理商）共享，合同义务要求其保护您的数据
  • 法律要求 — 法律、法院命令或政府机构要求时
  • 安全保障 — 为保护 Mythal、我们的用户或公众的权利、财产或安全
  • 业务转让 — 与合并、收购或资产出售相关

5. 数据保留

我们在您的账户活跃期间或提供服务所需的期限内保留您的信息。具体而言：

  • 账户数据 — 账户活跃期间保留
  • 聊天历史 — 在每次对话期间保留，受上下文窗口限制约束
  • 交易记录 — 按财务法规要求至少保留 7 年
  • AI 使用记录 — 保留用于计费审计和服务改进

删除账户后，我们将在 30 天内删除您的个人数据，法律要求保留的除外。

6. 数据安全

我们采用行业标准安全措施保护您的信息：

  • 所有传输中的数据均使用 TLS/SSL 加密
  • 密码经过哈希处理，从不以明文存储
  • 认证令牌安全管理，具有自动过期机制
  • 个人数据的访问仅限于授权人员

但是，互联网传输或电子存储没有 100% 安全的方法。虽然我们努力使用商业上可接受的手段保护您的信息，但无法保证绝对安全。

7. 您的权利

根据您所在的司法管辖区，您可能拥有以下权利：

  • 访问权 — 请求获取我们持有的关于您的个人数据副本
  • 更正权 — 请求更正不准确的数据
  • 删除权 — 请求删除您的个人数据（通过应用内的"删除账户"功能）
  • 可携权 — 请求以机器可读格式获取您的数据
  • 撤回同意 — 您可以随时停止使用本服务并删除账户

如需行使这些权利，请联系 support@mythal.app。

8. 未成年人隐私

本服务面向 18 岁及以上用户。我们不会有意收集未满 18 周岁未成年人的个人信息。如果我们发现收集了未满 18 周岁未成年人的信息，将立即删除。

如果您认为我们无意中收集了未成年人的信息，请联系 support@mythal.app。

9. 国际数据传输

您的信息可能被传输到您所在国家/地区以外的国家/地区并进行处理。我们的服务器位于美国。使用本服务即表示您同意将数据传输到美国及我们服务提供商运营的其他司法管辖区。

我们确保国际传输符合适用的数据保护法律，并已采取适当的保障措施。

10. 第三方服务

本服务集成了以下第三方服务，每项服务均受其自身隐私政策约束：

  • Apple App Store — 用于应用分发和应用内购买
  • 通过 Apple 登录 — 用于 Apple ID 认证
  • Google 登录 — 用于 Google 账户认证
  • AI 模型提供商 — 用于内容生成（文本、图片、视频、语音）
  • 云托管 — 用于服务器基础设施和数据存储

我们不对这些第三方的隐私实践负责。建议您查阅他们的隐私政策。

11. Cookie 与追踪

Mythal 移动应用不使用浏览器 Cookie。我们可能会使用设备标识符和分析令牌用于应用性能监控和崩溃报告。

我们不在移动应用中使用第三方广告 SDK 或跨应用追踪。

12. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。重大变更将通过应用内通知或电子邮件告知。顶部的"生效日期"标明了最新修订的时间。

变更生效后继续使用本服务即构成对更新后政策的接受。
