1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address — for account creation and authentication (only if you register by email)
  • Apple ID subject identifier — for Sign in with Apple. We store only the opaque subject identifier issued by Apple, never your Apple password
  • Google ID subject identifier — only for accounts that previously registered with Google Sign-In. Google Sign-In is not currently offered as a registration option in the app, but the identifier is retained for those existing accounts so they can still sign in
  • Password — stored only as a salted cryptographic hash; never in plaintext and never recoverable by us
  • Nickname — your display name within the app
  • Language preference — for interface localization
  • Profile details you choose to provide — avatar image, self-description, gender, and an age range you select during age verification

1.2 Age Verification Data

Because Mythal contains adult content, we verify that you are at least 18 before granting access. We collect:

  • Self-declared age range — one of a fixed set of bands (18–22, 23–25, 26–30, 31–35, 35+). We do not require and do not ask for your exact date of birth
  • Verification record — the method used and the timestamp of verification

1.3 Content You Create

  • Characters you create — names, short bios, backgrounds, opening messages, tags, and the AI-generated portrait images you produce and choose to keep. These are stored on our servers so that public characters can be discovered by other users
  • Feedback you submit — the text and any screenshots or images you attach when reporting a problem

Personas are stored only on your device. The user-side personas you define for yourself in role-play — including their names, descriptions and persona images — are kept in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or to object storage. When you chat, a persona's text is included in the prompt and transmitted for that request only, exactly as described for messages in Section 1.4.

1.4 Conversation Data — Important

Our servers do not store your chat messages. This is a deliberate architectural choice:

  • Your prompts and the AI's responses are transmitted to our server only for the duration of the request, so that they can be forwarded to an AI model provider and streamed back to you. They are not written to any server-side database.
  • Conversation history is stored only on your own device, in the app's local database. If you delete the app or its data, that history is gone and we cannot recover it for you.
  • Because we do not retain messages server-side, we cannot disclose, export, or restore your conversation history in response to a data request. A copy of your conversations exists only on your device.
  • Conversation metadata is retained server-side — for example the existence of a chat session, its title, the model used, and timestamps. Metadata contains neither your message text nor the AI's responses.

Our production server logs likewise do not contain the text of your messages or the AI's replies. Logs record operational signals only (request identifiers, model names, latency, token counts, error codes).

1.5 Voice Input

  • When you use press-and-hold voice input, speech recognition is performed by the operating system's own speech framework (Apple Speech on iOS). Mythal receives the resulting text, not an audio recording.
  • We do not record, store, or transmit your voice. Any audio processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.
  • Character voice playback (text-to-speech) synthesizes audio from text on request. We do not collect or clone your voice, and we do not build a voiceprint from it.

1.6 Transaction Data

  • Purchase records — Apple App Store transaction identifiers, product identifiers, amounts, currency, and order status, used to verify and fulfil purchases
  • Receipt data — the Apple transaction payload, retained so we can re-verify subscription renewals and process refunds
  • Virtual currency ledger — your Gem balance and a record of each credit and debit
  • We do not receive or store your credit card number, bank details, or any other payment instrument. All payment processing is handled by Apple.

1.7 Technical Data

  • IP address — used for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and to select a region-appropriate AI model workspace. We use it to infer an approximate country, not a precise location
  • Country code — derived from your device's region setting (not from GPS), used to select regional compliance rules
  • Time zone — derived from your device, used for daily check-in scheduling
  • Operating system name and app language tag — sent with each request for compatibility and localization
  • HTTP user agent string — received automatically by any web server, used for debugging
  • Usage metrics — AI model names used, token counts, Gem costs, feature interactions, and error reports. These are aggregate operational figures and do not include the content of your messages

We do not collect: precise GPS location, your contacts, your photo library beyond images you explicitly upload, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any cross-app tracking identifier.

3. AI Processing & Your Data

When you interact with AI characters, your message text is transmitted to third-party AI model providers so that a response can be generated. These providers act as our service providers — they process your content on our instructions to deliver the feature you requested:

  • Text generation — Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen family) and OpenRouter-routed open models, via OpenAI-compatible APIs
  • Image generation — Alibaba Cloud (Wan), xAI (Grok Imagine), and OpenRouter-routed models (FLUX)
  • Video generation — third-party AI video model providers
  • Speech synthesis (TTS) — third-party text-to-speech providers
  • Cloud infrastructure and object storage — for hosting and for storing images you generate

What we send: the content of your prompt, the conversation context needed to generate a coherent reply, and — for image generation — reference images such as your character's portrait and, if you have set one, your own avatar. We do not send your email address, nickname, password, payment data, or other account identifiers to these providers.

Provider data practices: each provider processes this content under its own privacy policy and terms. We prefer providers whose published terms state that API inputs are not used to train their models, and we select for that where the option exists. However, we do not control these providers' internal processing, and we cannot give you an absolute guarantee about their retention or handling. Where a provider's terms permit retention for abuse monitoring, your prompt content may be held by that provider for a limited period under its own policy. You should assume that anything you type into a conversation may be read by the provider's systems.

Consequence for you: do not include in your prompts any information you would not want a third-party AI provider to process — in particular, do not include real people's names, private identifying details, or information about third parties who have not consented.

5.1 What account deletion removes

When you use Delete Account in the app, deletion is immediate — not deferred. We then:

  • Irreversibly anonymize your account record: email, nickname, avatar, password hash, gender, age range, self-description, and social sign-in identifiers are cleared, and the account is marked deleted so it can no longer be used to sign in
  • Delete your characters, chat sessions, relationships, activity records, feedback, age verification record, daily check-ins, task records, and AI usage metrics
  • Delete your uploaded and generated image and video files from object storage
  • Revoke all authentication tokens, signing you out on every device

Because your personas and conversation history are stored only on your device, they are outside our deletion process — they are removed when you delete the app or clear its data, and we hold no server-side copy of either.

The following are deliberately retained after account deletion, in a form no longer linked to any identifying detail about you:

Legal basis for retention: these records are kept to comply with tax, accounting, and consumer-protection law, to defend or resolve payment disputes and chargebacks, and to process refunds and subscription reconciliations that may arrive after deletion. This falls within the exceptions to the right to delete under CCPA §1798.105(d)(1) (complying with a legal obligation) and §1798.105(d)(3) (detecting and protecting against fraudulent or illegal activity). After the 7-year period these records are deleted.

Unpaid subscription: deleting your Mythal account does not cancel an App Store subscription. You must cancel separately in your Apple subscription settings to avoid further charges. The app reminds you of this on the deletion screen.

6. Data Security

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.4 Inferences and Content Classification
