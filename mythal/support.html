Contact Us

For any question or issue — account, purchase, content report, or privacy — email us at support@mythal.app. Include your account email and, for purchase issues, the order or transaction ID from your Apple receipt.

We are an online-only service; email is our official support channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, select Mythal Premium Monthly, and choose a new period or Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium benefits until the end of the paid period. You can also manage it from the App Store app on any device signed in with your Apple Account.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, go to Profile → More → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent — no email required. Your account and its stored data are removed right away, and any active subscription must be cancelled separately in your Apple Account settings.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Cashier in the app (Profile → Upgrade) and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account — make sure the app is signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

I was charged but did not receive my Gems or Premium.

First tap Restore Purchases in the Cashier. Entitlements are normally granted within minutes of purchase confirmation. If the problem persists, email support@mythal.app with the transaction ID from your Apple receipt.

How do I request a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly, but we are happy to help investigate any billing problem.

How do I report inappropriate content?

In the app, long-press any chat message and select Report, or use the Report option on a character or a comment in its comments section. Reports are reviewed and actioned within 24 hours. You can also email support@mythal.app.

Is Mythal for adults only?

Yes. Mythal is intended for adults aged 18 and over and contains mature fictional role-play content. Age verification is required during onboarding, and users who indicate they are under 18 cannot access the service.

What are Gems?

Gems are the in-app currency used for AI conversations and generated content. You receive Gems from daily check-ins, as part of a Premium subscription, or by purchasing Gem packs in the Cashier. Gem balances and usage history are visible in the app under Profile → Wallet.

Where can I read your policies?

See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. For privacy requests, email support@mythal.app with the subject line "Privacy Request".

常见问题

如何管理或取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理。打开 设置 → 您的 Apple 账户 → 订阅，选择 Mythal Premium Monthly，即可更换周期或取消订阅。取消后，Premium 权益保留至当期结束。您也可以在任何登录同一 Apple 账户的设备上通过 App Store 应用管理。

如何删除账户与数据？

在应用内进入 个人中心 → 更多 → 注销账户，立即生效、永久删除，无需发邮件。账户及其存放的数据会被即刻移除；如有进行中的订阅，请另行在 Apple 账户设置中取消。

如何恢复已购买的权益？

打开应用内收银台（个人中心 → 升级），点击 恢复购买。购买与您的 Apple 账户绑定——请确认应用登录的是购买时使用的同一 Apple 账户。

已扣款但没有收到宝石或 Premium 权益。

请先在收银台点击 恢复购买；权益通常在购买确认后几分钟内自动发放。若仍未到账，请发邮件至 support@mythal.app，附上 Apple 收据中的交易号。

如何申请退款？

所有支付由 Apple 处理，退款请通过 reportaproblem.apple.com 向 Apple 申请。我们无法直接退款，但乐意协助排查任何账单问题。

如何举报不当内容？

在应用内长按任意聊天消息，选择 举报；或在角色及其评论区的 举报 入口提交。举报将在 24 小时内处理。您也可以发邮件至 support@mythal.app。

Mythal 仅限成年人使用吗？

是的。Mythal 仅面向 18 岁及以上用户，包含成人向虚构角色扮演内容。首次使用时必须完成年龄验证；选择未满 18 岁的用户无法访问服务。

什么是宝石（Gems）？

宝石是应用内用于 AI 对话与生成内容的代币。您可以通过每日签到、Premium 订阅月赠或在收银台购买宝石包获得。宝石余额与使用记录可在应用内 个人中心 → 钱包 查看。

在哪里查看协议条款？

请参阅我们的 用户协议 与 隐私政策。隐私请求请发邮件至 support@mythal.app，主题写明"Privacy Request"。
